Change The limit for concurrent VMs per job now varies based on the number of zones allowed for a job's VMs: The limit for concurrent VMs per single-zone job remains 2,000. The limit for concurrent VMs per multi-zone job has increased to 4,000. Learn more about Quotas and limits and Batch locations.
