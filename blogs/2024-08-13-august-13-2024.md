Change The documentation has been updated to clarify that a Batch OS stops being supported when its base Compute Engine OS is deprecated. This restriction only applies to Batch OSes that have not already reached the end of development as of the date of this notice. For more information, see Restrictions for VM OS images. Deprecated Batch CentOS (batch-centos) and Batch HPC CentOS (batch-hpc-centos) have reached end of development due to the end of support (EOS) of Compute Engine CentOS 7 images...
