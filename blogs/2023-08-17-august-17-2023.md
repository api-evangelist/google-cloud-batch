Breaking Batch is enforcing a 60-day retention policy for all finished (failed or succeeded) jobs: Any existing jobs that have finished before August 17, 2023 are automatically deleted 60 days after, on October 16, 2023. All new and existing jobs that are not yet finished on August 17, 2023 are automatically deleted 60 days after they finish running. If you need to retain the information for a job for more than 60 days, you can export the job.
