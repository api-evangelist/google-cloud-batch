Breaking Starting on the following dates, you can no longer create a job that locates its Compute Engine resources outside of the job's location. For projects that have successfully submitted before July 31, 2026 at least one job that uses the allowedLocations[] field with any region or zones outside of the job's location, changes are starting on June 30, 2027 . For all other projects, changes are starting on July 31, 2026 .
