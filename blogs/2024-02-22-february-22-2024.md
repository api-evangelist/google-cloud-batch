Change In the Google Cloud console, the Job list page has been updated to reduce latency. Although the console no longer summarizes the statuses of your jobs, you can filter based on job state when you view a list of your jobs. Fixed Fixed the issue causing latency when listing jobs in projects that contain more than 10,000 jobs.
